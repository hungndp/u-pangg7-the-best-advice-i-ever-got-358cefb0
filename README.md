Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Best Advice I Ever Got"](https://make.fun/c/u-pangg7-the-best-advice-i-ever-got-358cefb0) — one sentence, no longer than 200 characters, about the best advice ever received.

## The sentence

> My father, packing his toolbox the night he lost the factory job, said, "Son, never let a bad day decide who you are," and I have carried that sentence through every bad day since.

180 characters. One concrete moment — a father, a toolbox, the night he lost his factory job — the advice voiced exactly as it was given, and a last clause that proves it was earned: the line has been carried through every bad day since.

## How it works

`main.py` writes exactly that one sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (no trailing newline, so the byte count is the sentence itself). The program is deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
